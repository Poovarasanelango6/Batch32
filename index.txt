44.What is the difference between let and const?
    variables declared with let can be reassigned a new value, while variables declared with const cannot be reassigned.

45.What is the difference between var and const?
    var is function-scoped and can be updated and re-declared, while const is block-scoped, cannot be re-declared, and cannot be reassigned.
